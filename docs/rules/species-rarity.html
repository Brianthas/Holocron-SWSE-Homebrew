Common

  • Humans are perhaps the single most common sight in the galaxy. They are found on nearly every planet in the discovered regions of the galaxy. They're also one of the most likely species to venture off into the unknown so you can never rule them out there either.
  • Caamasi, Duros, Rodian, Sullustan, Togruta, Trandoshan, Twi'lek, Zabrak, Zeltron are also very common sights in the galaxy. They're found throughout the galaxy operating at nearly every level of any kind of society imaginable.
