Program Core

Each droid comes with a preprogrammed core which forms the basis for its personality when first activated. This is also the default it will be restored back to if it has its memory wiped. Droids with Heuristic Processors can learn and grow, therefore can gain traits and programming flaws beyond what their original programming would otherwise allow.

Each Droid will fall into one of the following Classes, also called Degrees, and will never move out of it.

CLASS ABILITY SCORES
One Intelligence or Wisdom
Two Intelligence
Three Charisma
Four Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution
Five Strength or Constitution
These are not requirements, merely recommendations.
  • Please note that unless the Droid is Class Four, it is unable to harm sentients unless the Droid gains Sapience.

Class One - Analysis

Class One Droids are typically of higher intelligence and possess one or more specialized skills. Many computational computers, when put into a Droid chassis, also fit into Class One.

Examples:
Administration, Analysis, Archive, Biological Sciences, Communications, Interrogation, Mathematics, Medical, and Physical Sciences.

Class Two - Technical

Class Two Droids are typically proficient at highly technical skills, including mechanical and engineering disciplines. Class Two Droids are rarely equipped with Basic vocabulators, instead communicating through Binary.

Examples:
Agromech, Astromech, Engineering, Environmental, Exploration, Maintenance, Mechanic, Network Security, Observation, Pilot, Repair, Slicing, Tech, and Utility.

Class Three - Protocol

Class Three Droids are programmed for interaction with Sentient species and thus almost always have both vocabulators and at least an imitation of a personality to interact with.

Examples:
Administrative, Assistant, Bureaucrat, Child Care, Controller, Espionage, Gatekeeper, Hospitality, Interaction, Interpreter, Luxury, Messenger, Protocol, Secretary, Servant, Service, Supervisor, Tutor, and Valet.

Class Four - Combat

Class Four Droids are programmed to fight. By default they are the only Droids that can harm a sentient creature. They are also believed to be the oldest category of Droids.

Examples:
Artillery, Assassin, Assault, Battle, Combat, Commando, Crab, Destroyer, Gladiatorial, Guard, Guardian, Hunter, Infantry, Infiltrator, Juggernaut, Killer, Legionnaire, Patrol, Police, Probe, Recon, Sabotage, Security, Seeker, Sentinel, Sentry, Spider, Tactical, Training, Turret, War

Class Five - Labour

Class Five Droids are designed for repetitive work, menial labour, and hazardous jobs. Class Five Droids, similar to Class Two, are rarely equipped with Basic vocabulators, instead communicating through Binary. Along with this Class Five Droids are the least likely to be equipped with heuristic processors and were considered the most interchangeable and replaceable.

Examples:
Construction, Demolitions, Excavator, Hazard, Labour, Loader, Lifter, Maintenance, Miner, Minesweeper, Ordnance, Power, Sifter, Smelter, Surveyor, Utility, Worker

Talent Trees

Droids can select from all Droid-Degree talent trees as long as it makes sense for their design and character.

Sapience

In all droids, sapience can be considered a programming quirk occurring when something goes wrong. Sapience in droids only happens when a droid, possessing a heuristic processor, gains the ability to set new goals in order to achieve their own ends or goals unrelated to their original programming. Frequently it is both. One of the most common types of of anomalies manifests in the ability to harm other sapient creatures (in all droid classes). How much they may adhere or reject their original programming varies from droid to droid. Rarely is all elements of their original program gone. Most often it is just distorted, such as in a logical paradox where one part of their programming conflicts with another party. Though there are the rare examples when the Droid has been mistreated or where it has desires that do not seem to logically follow from their base programming.

Only Droid-PCs and Independent Droids possess Sapience by default.

Systems

  • Do not factor in frame and locomotion elements into carrying capacity.
  • Each System takes up, at least, one Light slot per system.
    • Hidden Systems take up twice as many slots as normal.
  • Locomotion:
    • Walking operates as if the Droid is an organic being of the Droid's size.
    • Hovering operates at a reduced speed (-5 feet). This does not allow them to levitate at extreme heights.
    • Wheeled provides a boost to speed (+10 feet). Droids with Wheeled locomotion may have difficulties with stairs but they may gain bonuses in other circumstances.
  • Droids may only have one modified system per accessory.
    • Example: A Droid may possess magnetic feet or climbing claws, not both.
    • Example: A Droid may have an improved sensor package or darkvision, not both.
  • Translator Units cost 10 000 credits and effectively considers the Intelligence of the Droid to be 4 higher for the purposes of understanding languages.
    • Assuming the Droid can speak, it also grants the ability to speak one other language (usually Basic, Huttese, or Ryl).
    • This item does stack with Linguist. Meaning Droids with Linguistics and intelligence score of 10 will often speak all three trade languages (Basic, Huttese, and Ryl).
    • Individual translation packs cost 100 credits modified by rarity and must be installed one at a time taking one hour per language.
  • Vocabulators cost 2 000 and allows a Droid to speak any language the Droid knows.
    • Without a Vocabulator they can only speak Binary.
    • Once a Droid has a Vocabulator they are incapable of speaking Binary. As such they are granted another language as their default Species Language, as determined by their manufacturer or the Vocabulator manufacturer.
  • Upgrades from the Tech Specialist or Superior Tech abilities do not take up slots.
  • Integrated equipment takes up twice as many slots as non-integrated equipment.

Disallowed

  • Specialized Sub-Processors.
  • Hardened Systems.
  • Shield Generators.
  • Droid Manufacturer Traits.
