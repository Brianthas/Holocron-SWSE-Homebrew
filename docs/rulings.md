# Rulings

Clarifications to the rules in `docs/rules/`, made by Bryan (with tovec where noted). Where a ruling
and a snapshot differ, the ruling wins. Code and tests cite the ruling by its heading.

## Talent trees

- **Force Sensitivity** grants the general Force talent trees: Alter, Control, Dark Side, Guardian
  Spirit, Light Side, Midi-chlorian, Sense. (The packs' "Force Talent Trees" minus "Force Tradition
  Talent Trees".) Anyone may take Force Sensitivity.
- **Jedi Order** grants Jedi Consular, Jedi Guardian and Jedi Sentinel.
- **Another tradition** replaces those three with the tradition's tree. Where the tradition lists
  several ("Believer Disciple, Blazing Chain, or Krath"), the character picks one. Traditions other
  than the Jedi Order need GM approval; species limits warn and never block.
- **Lightsaber Combat** stays open to every Jedi, whatever the tradition. (Bryan and tovec,
  2026-09-23.)
- **Ithorian Priesthood** is not offered: no tree exists for it.
- Trees can be added or removed on a character by hand; each hand edit is marked.

## Combat

- No Condition Track. Damage at or above Damage Threshold does nothing extra.
- **DT+5** is Fortitude Defense + 5, and is the defense against forced movement, grapple and disarm.
- Bonuses of the same type do not stack; the highest applies. Untyped bonuses stack.
- Critical hits multiply by 2 unless a weapon or feat says otherwise. A natural 1 always misses and
  a natural 20 always hits.
- Half level is added to damage on attacks.
- Armor has no armor bonus to Reflex or Fortitude. It replaces the Dexterity term of Reflex: Assault
  uses the Strength modifier, Battle 4, Mesh 5, Power 4.

## Skills

- Skill total: half level (rounded down) + 5 if trained + 5 for Skill Focus + ability modifier.
- Trained skills available: the class taken at level 1 + Intelligence modifier + species features
  (bonus trained skills, Primitive +1) + feats (Skill Training).

## Hit points and healing

- Level 1: the class's level-1 hit points + Constitution modifier.
- Later levels: roll the class die + Constitution modifier, or take floor(die / 2) + 1 +
  Constitution modifier.
- Second Wind: 1 use unless a feat adds more; heals Hit Die + Constitution modifier + level.

## Character creation

- 25 point buy with Pathfinder costs: 8 -2, 9 -1, 10 0, 11 1, 12 2, 13 3, 14 5, 15 7, 16 10, 17 13,
  18 17. A score below 8 refunds the same as 8.

## The Force

- Force Points per day: by level, +1 for Force Sensitivity, +2 for Force Boon.
- A Force Point spent before a roll: roll 2d20 and keep the higher. Spent after: reroll and take the
  new roll.
- Force of Personality uses Charisma instead of Wisdom for Will Defense. The Episode VII line saying
  it swaps the Force ability is a typo.
- Charismatic Force uses Charisma instead of Wisdom for Use the Force. (Text to follow.)
- Move Object: the check sets the largest size you can lift. Damage is set by the size of what is
  moved (Medium 2d6, Large 4d6, Huge 6d6, Gargantuan 8d6, Colossal 10d6), not by the check.

## Equipment

- Only the weapons in the Episode VII weapon tables exist.
