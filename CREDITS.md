## Compendium content

The compendium packs (`packs/_source` and the built packs) are converted from the packs of
[SWSE: Homebrew Edition](https://github.com/Brianthas/SWSE-Homebrew-Edition), a fork of
[Foundry-VTT-StarWars-SagaEdition](https://github.com/kypvalanx/Foundry-VTT-StarWars-SagaEdition)
by Andy Lijewski (kypvalanx), whose item text comes from the
[Star Wars Saga Edition Wiki](https://swse.fandom.com/). That content is licensed under
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), and the converted packs are
released under the same licence. The converter (`tools/convert/`) changes it: house rules applied,
fan content left out, official errata applied, and text cleaned of wiki markup.
